About Me

School Years

In school, studying was more of a responsibility than a passion. Passing exams was necessary, while scoring high marks was treated as a luxury. I was often reminded about rewards for good marks and the promise of a better future. This mindset remained until 11th grade, when I watched the movie Mission Mangal. The film inspired me to look at education differently and sparked a strong interest in space and science. I began to see learning as a journey driven by curiosity and creativity rather than only a way to secure jobs or money.

Shaping My Thinking

Science showed me that everything has a deeper essence, whether in nature or in other forms. This way of thinking influenced my personality and later became a key part of my programming and development approach. Alongside this, I was always a tech enthusiast. I enjoyed creating as much as consuming, from recording and editing videos to posting them online. Over the years, exploring such diverse content sharpened creativity as one of my strongest skills.

The Role of Technology

The growth of online education, especially YouTube, and now the rise of AI, gave me access to endless opportunities to explore. My interest in technology made it possible to learn and grow without depending on slow and traditional methods like scanning lengthy textbooks or waiting for guidance. These resources opened doors that shaped both my knowledge and my curiosity.

Engineering Journey

The early years of engineering often felt stressful. Assignments and projects seemed demanding and at times unachievable. However, choosing computer science in 11th and 12th gave me an advantage when it came to coding. While many others struggled, I was able to build on my foundation. I often pushed myself to take extra steps or follow unique approaches, even when I knew I might be the only one doing so. These efforts paid off and taught me that beginnings are difficult, but with time challenges become far more manageable.

Learning and Growth

I see learning as one of the most powerful skills. Its value may not be visible immediately, but with patience and resilience it creates opportunities over time. The project based learning approach made this even clearer. It does not stop at memorizing concepts but also highlights why knowledge matters and how it is applied. It also exposes students to deadlines, teamwork, and real-world constraints, which build skills that go far beyond academics.

Outlook

To me, success is about inner peace. Achieving it requires discipline, resilience, and a continuous willingness to learn. At present, I focus on creating projects related to websites and mobile apps, driven by the belief that technology has the power to bring meaningful change and lasting value.

Service Bazaar

A prototype mobile app connecting users with local service providers.

  • Service Bazaar: End-to-End, Multi-Functional Service Marketplace and Supply Chain Platform
  • Service Bazaar is a full-stack, two-sided marketplace application built to streamline local service booking and integrate the sale of related supplies. The entire platform operates on a React Native mobile client communicating with a Node.js/Express API layer. All structured and transactional data—including users, bookings, inventory, and chat history—is managed efficiently using a highly scalable and flexible Firebase NoSQL database.
  • Advanced Location-Driven Service Discovery: The platform's core booking intelligence is driven by user location. It utilizes Expo Location to obtain user coordinates, followed by a Nominatim reverse geocoding approach to provide human-readable location names (locationDisplayName). The ProvidersScreen implements filtering and sorting based on proximity to the user's selected location, which is manageable via an interactive WebView map interface.
  • Complex Booking Logic and Lifecycle Management: A tightly validated, multi-step booking process (BookingModal.js) that dynamically checks a provider’s real-time schedule, ensuring the user’s selected date, time, and custom duration are non-conflicting. Service integrity is enforced through an OTP-based confirmation protocol on the BookingScreen.js, which is mandatory for the service state to transition to double confirmed. Post-service completion triggers a mandatory rating and review modal to maintain service quality and update provider statistics.
  • Real-Time & Communication Infrastructure: Integrated Socket.IO into the Node.js server to enable low-latency, real-time chat between users and providers. This chat module (BookingChatScreen.js) not only handles standard text messages but also supports the recording, encoding, and playback of voice messages using Expo-AV and Base64 data transfer. This enhances communication beyond basic text.
  • Integrated E-commerce and Order Fulfillment: The platform includes a dedicated Supplies section for product sales. The purchase flow includes robust client-side cart management with AsyncStorage. The CheckoutScreen.js calculates a mandatory 18% tax (GST) on the subtotal and handles order submission. Order statuses are tracked through the OrderHistoryScreen, and products can be rated upon delivery.
  • Cross-Language Administrative Tooling: The project demonstrates strong versatility through the creation of dedicated Python-based administrative tools. The applications viewer.py uses the Tkinter GUI toolkit and Firebase Admin SDK to allow admins to visually review, approve, or reject new provider applications, seamlessly updating user and provider records in Firestore. Additionally, backup data.py performs a full recursive export of the Firebase Firestore collections to JSON files for disaster recovery, proving proficiency in system maintenance and data integrity.
